Privacy Policy for Build Tower

Effective date: 27 August 2026
Developer: Oleksandr Sanin
Contact: saninalexsanin@gmail.com

Summary

Build Tower does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline on your device. There is no account, no login, no server, and no analytics.

Information we do not collect

Build Tower does not collect any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other contact details
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses
  • Location data
  • Photos, files, contacts, or calendar data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioural telemetry
  • Payment or financial information

Data stored on your device

To let you continue playing where you left off, Build Tower saves game state locally on your device only. This includes your level progress and earned awards, in-game currency and purchased in-game items, your daily play streak, and your sound and language preferences.

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, is not accessible to the developer, and is not shared with any third party. It is not linked to your identity.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Build Tower permanently removes all locally stored game data. On Android you can also clear it without uninstalling, via Settings → Apps → Build Tower → Storage → Clear data.

Permissions

Build Tower does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or files. The app plays sound effects and uses device vibration, neither of which requires access to your personal information.

Third parties

Build Tower contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, no social network integrations, and no in-app purchases. No third-party service receives data from this app.

Children

Build Tower is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information about a child can be gathered, stored, or disclosed through it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Since the app collects no data, any change is expected to be clarifying rather than substantive.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in Build Tower can be sent to saninalexsanin@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the Build Tower mobile application distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play.
